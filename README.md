## As an Occupational Therapist and enthusiast for Australian architectural history, the old Kenmore Hospital is a fascination for me. As an institution, its touched a great many lives and seen so much community interaction with Goulburn over the years. 

## I’ve read through its history at the local Goulburn War Memorial and spent time on the outskirts of the site, wondering what’s going on and why this marvellous site is deteriorating in the face of so much community need for housing, homelessness support and community interaction.

# Can Kenmore Be More?

As an Occupational Therapist, my profession’s genesis lay in the care and rehabilitation of returning veterans, forever transformed by their war experience, either physically, psychologically, or both; at sites like Kenmore, so its clinical heritage is also noteworthy. You can read some of my initial thoughts on its fate in From Kenmore to NoMore? — Laughing Mind

I’ve started the process of exploring in more depth through Council and the Goulburn community to better understand some of the stories of the Kenmore site. This online presence is a container for some of that activity, to help gather media articles and stories of the site, assess community sentiment and express some of the hopes, dreams and possibilities for the site.

I welcome visitors to this initiative and would love to hear from you if you have a personal or professional history of the site. There is interesting material in related academic research that documents the evolution of clinical care at the site, but institutions like this are far more than that, with sporting events held, skills learnt, time spent on the grounds. Sadly, the site is developing a reputation in online platforms that attracts a destructive level of intrusion and vandalism that is accelerating its decline.

My background is as a health professional and technology project manager but with added skills and experience from Australian Army Engineers Corp and a long family history of service in local government engineering, with many other extended family members involved in healthcare and nursing. My companies have won regional and national innovation awards, I’ve helped clients achieve similar outcomes and can see so much potential for the old Kenmore site. I’ve helped develop and deliver large scale property and security management systems that I know can assist with protection of the site, tracking assets and building conditions whilst planning and renovation works continue. It’s important to not see its condition deteriorate further. Site activations can help address some of this through the simple presence of people and extra eyes, amongst other considerations.
